Welcome to NA Group

NA Group comprises a group of production and trading companies in Asia and Europe. Its strategy is based on supplying components and finished products from the Far East and Eastern Europe to the manufacturing industry.

Our approach to advice and support is based on simple, direct and personal contact with our customers. All subsidiaries have grown on their own strength – without bank loans, with profits reinvested in NA Group.

We don't just offer products – through partnership we create added value for our customers. We warmly invite you to become part of our big family!
