STAN MK

Prilep, North Macedonia

Part of NA Group since 2015

STAN MK was founded in 2015 and specialises in supplying the European bedding and mattress industry. The portfolio comprises zippers, spacer fabrics, mattress covers and zipper bags.

The company is located in Prilep, one of the largest centres in the region. Thanks to its favourable location between Europe, the Middle East and the Far East, markets can be served optimally from here; labour costs allow competitive prices.
