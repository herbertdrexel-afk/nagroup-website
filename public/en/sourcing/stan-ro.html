STAN RO

Sibiu, Romania

Part of NA Group since 2013

STAN RO has been part of NA Group since 2013 and specialises in delivering from its warehouse in Romania – components such as zippers and spacer fabrics for the mattress industry. The company also produces garden cushions and protective covers.

Main products in stock: zippers, 3D mesh fabric in full rolls and 3D mesh fabric borders. On request we supply any other products our group partners can develop.

STAN RO is located in Sibiu, one of Romania's most important cultural centres and European Capital of Culture in 2007. The company is regarded as a reliable industry partner – with professionalism, short delivery times and competitive prices.
