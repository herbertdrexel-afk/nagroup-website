STAN Asia

Shanghai, China

Part of NA Group since 2003

STAN Asia has been a member of NA Group since 2003. The company specialises in supplying the mattress industry with components such as zippers and spacer fabrics. In addition, it built up a supply chain of protective materials to fight pandemics within a very short time.

STAN Asia is located in Shanghai – a city that has blossomed anew in recent decades and is now one of the best-known cities in the world. The name means “on the sea”; Shanghai is also called the “Pearl of the Orient”.
